EU Privacy Regulators Report Record Number of Cookie Consent Violations in 2026

February 13, 2026 · 4 min read · By Sarah Mitchell
[ Article Image ]

European data protection authorities have collectively issued a record number of enforcement actions related to cookie consent mechanisms, with dark patterns in consent banners being the primary focus of regulatory scrutiny this year.

The trend highlights growing frustration among regulators with websites that make rejecting cookies deliberately difficult — hiding reject buttons, using confusing language, or requiring multiple clicks to opt out while offering a single prominent "Accept All" button.

"Users should be able to reject non-essential cookies as easily as they can accept them," said the European Data Protection Board in a statement released alongside the enforcement figures. "Any implementation that makes rejection harder than acceptance is a clear violation of GDPR principles."
